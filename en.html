FinanzAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (app version 1.1.0)

FinanzAI ("the App") is a personal finance app available for Android and as a web app (including iPhone). This policy explains which data is processed and how, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Google Play data safety requirements.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the developer of FinanzAI. For any privacy request: marco.p.develop@gmail.com.

2. Using the App without an account: data stays on the device

The App also works without an account. In that case transactions, budgets, goals, categories, loyalty cards and preferences stay only on your device and are not sent to the developer.

3. Optional account and sync

If you create an account (optional), the following is processed:

  • email and password (the password is stored only in hashed form) to sign in;
  • the display name you choose, shown to the other members of your wallets;
  • your personal financial data (transactions, budgets, goals, categories, personal loyalty cards) and preferences (theme, currency, notifications, reminder time and time zone), to keep them in sync across your devices;
  • a password recovery code, stored only as a hash (not readable).

Data is stored on Supabase (servers in the European Union, Ireland), acting as data processor. Connections are always encrypted (HTTPS) and row-level access rules make sure each user only sees their own data.

4. Shared wallets and shared loyalty cards

If you create a wallet or join one with an invite code, the wallet's expenses (description, amount, date, who paid and how it is split) and the loyalty cards you choose to share (store and card number) are visible to all members of that wallet, together with your display name. You can leave a wallet at any time; expenses already recorded remain visible to the other members.

5. Notifications

Notifications are optional and enabled only after your explicit choice. On Android they are generated on the device. On the web/iPhone, if you enable them with an account, a technical push subscription identifier (Web Push) is stored so that reminders and wallet updates can be delivered.

6. Usage statistics and errors (diagnostics)

To improve the App and fix problems, the App sends the developer technical information:

  • the type of action performed (e.g. "sign-in", "expense added", "wallet created"), without any content: never amounts, descriptions, expense or store names, card numbers, invite codes or typed emails;
  • the App's technical error messages;
  • platform (Android, iPhone, web), App version and a random installation identifier (generated by the App, not tied to hardware or advertising);
  • if you have an account, your display name, to link errors to the user who encounters them.

This data is kept for at most 90 days (unresolved errors until they are fixed) and is used only by the developer for analytics and support. It is never sold or shared.

7. What the App does NOT do

  • no advertising, no advertising or profiling SDKs;
  • no advertising ID;
  • no sale or transfer of data to third parties;
  • suggestions and statistics run entirely on the device, without external AI services.

8. Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a loyalty card's code. Images are neither stored nor sent: only the code number is read.

9. Manual backup

You can export your data to a JSON or CSV file. The file stays under your control: the developer has no access to it.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Without an account: data stays on the device until you delete it ("Delete all data") or uninstall the App.
  • With an account: you can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Account, synced personal data, preferences and push subscriptions are deleted immediately, and you are removed from all wallets (wallets with no remaining members are deleted).

11. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Many of these can be exercised directly in the App (export, edit, account deletion); for anything else write to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

12. Children

The App is not intended for children under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when new features are added. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy: marco.p.develop@gmail.com
